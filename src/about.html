Company Profile

Pioneering the Future of Autonomous Exploration

Thalaxis Technology develops autonomous surface vessels and deep submergence units for research, defense, and maritime operators worldwide.

Our purpose and market

Founded in 2025, Thalaxis Technology specializes in the design and manufacture of autonomous marine platforms capable of sustained operations in punishing marine environments. Our mission is to advance marine exploration and defense through indigenous engineering excellence.

Engineering capabilities

Our fabrication facility operates CNC machining centers for advanced composites and titanium alloy components, pressure testing chambers, and hydrostatic test tanks rated to extreme equivalent depth. Each platform undergoes rigorous operational stress testing before deployment.

Mission

To advance autonomous marine operations through indigenous engineering excellence and reliable platforms that operate safely in extreme environments.

Vision

To become the trusted global partner for defense and research institutions requiring autonomous surface and subsea systems.
