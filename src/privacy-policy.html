Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Draft Notice: This privacy policy is a standard corporate legal draft. It requires review by Thalaxis Technology's authorized legal counsel prior to formal publication and commercial operation.

1. Introduction

Thalaxis Technology (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal data collected through our website (https://www.thalaxistechnology.in). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website or submit inquiries through our contact and career forms.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you submit an enquiry, apply for an open position, or communicate with us directly. This may include:

  • Full name and professional title
  • Business email address and phone number
  • Organization or institution name
  • Enquiry details, message contents, and mission specifications
  • Resume/CV and professional background details submitted via career applications

3. Purpose of Processing

We process the information collected solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to technical inquiries and procurement requests regarding our submersible systems
  • Evaluating job applications and managing recruitment communications
  • Improving website functionality and technical performance
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

4. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses minimal, essential cookies required for core site navigation and security. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies or third-party behavioral advertising networks.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable legal, tax, or accounting requirements.

7. Contact for Privacy Enquiries

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact us at:
